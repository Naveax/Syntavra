## 6. Continuous-lowering rule

After a band is certified, do not freeze optimization forever. The offline/shadow floor search may probe lower again after enough new evidence exists.

Stop lowering when the next reduction worsens any of:

- verified solve rate;
- security/constraint compliance;
- exact recovery;
- provider cost per successful task;
- latency per successful task beyond the accepted objective;
- retry/recall/fallback burden.
